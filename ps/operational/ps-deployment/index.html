    Paramedic Specialist Deployment
    Decision Support Tool

    Background

    The CliniCall team is a provincial resource tasked with providing frontline clinical leadership, shared decision making, and risk mitigation support to protect BCEHS' people, patients, and infrastructure province-wide.

    The CliniCall deployment model drives clinical excellence, safety leadership, and operational agility within BCEHS. By integrating virtual clinical oversight through CliniCall with the dynamic use of Tango, the CliniCall team provides critical support where and when it is needed most. This framework aligns deployment practices with high-acuity demand, CBRNE and HAZMAT safety, and collaborative field support ensuring optimal care delivery and crew safety province-wide.

    Purpose

    This Decision Support Tool (DST) identifies the deployment framework for Paramedic Specialist resources, the circumstances necessitating a Paramedic Specialist response, and Paramedic Specialist dispatching.

    Scope

    This procedure applies to paramedics when working as Clinical Hub Paramedic Specialists within Provincial Clinical Operations.

    2. Procedure

    a. Paramedic Specialist Deployment Strategy

    i. Paramedic Specialists deploy as lone responders with the intent of providing advanced care and specialized technical coverage across the Metro Vancouver geographical area, with focused deployment in the Vancouver Coastal and northern Fraser health regions.

    ii. As a Provincial resource, specific deployments outside the standard operating area (Metro Vancouver) may be identified by CliniCall and must be coordinated with the responsible Charge Emergency Medical Dispatcher (CEMD) and Provincial Operations Manager (POM).

    iii. As part of CliniCall outreach, Paramedic Specialists may request to be deployed to areas outside of Metro Vancouver. The key objective is to provide an ACP resource for the duration of the shift and promote CliniCall services. These geographical areas may include eastern Fraser Valley, Sea to Sky and Sunshine Coast as examples. Requests for special deployments need to be approved by the Clinical Hub Manager in advance of deployment, with consideration given to operational costs. Additionally, local leadership of the desired area must be engaged to collaborate on the final decision.

    iv. Paramedic Specialist deployment provides additional resources in a layered response for clinical leadership, technical advice, and crew support, irrespective of existing Advanced Care Paramedic (ACP) resources and MPDS coding.

    b. Paramedic Specialist Response Guidelines & Duties

    i. Both in dispatch and on the street, the primary focus of the Paramedic Specialist is delivering advanced high-quality care to patients, ensuring both responder and patient safety, and providing support to staff in the following situations: 1. Events identified by the dispatch operations center as requiring advanced care paramedic response based on Clinical Response Model priority. For example, cardiac arrest, status seizure, multisystem trauma. 2. Complex urgent and primary care events that require advanced clinical assessment and shared decision making. 3. Complex events that require clinical leadership in collaboration with frontline leaders (On-Call Paramedic Supervisors OCPS) e.g., multi-patient or mass casualty events.

    ii. Paramedic Specialists are tasked as Safety Officers per Incident Command System Structure for chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, and explosive (CBRNE), and High-Risk Hazard (HRH) events.

    1. When dispatched in this capacity, the intent is to assume responsibility for safety on scene.
    2. In this role, the Paramedic Specialist hold final decisions on safety for staging, entry into scene, and decontamination of patients and equipment. See - BCEHS DST for Paramedic Specialist HAZMAT and HRH Review
    3. CliniCall is responsible for reviewing and recommending responses in this capacity.
    4. A Tango (PS-PRU) unit may be diverted off an event prior to arrival at the direction of CliniCall for all events captured above.

    iii. Paramedic Specialists may be layered as an additional resource to provide clinical support to crews attending both high acuity and low acuity calls, or clinically challenging events, including the following situations:

    1. New ACPs attending High-Acuity-Low-Occurrence (HALO) events.
    2. PRU and/or de-paired ACP members attending HALO events.
    3. PRU and/or de-paired ACP members performing high risk procedures.
    4. Any crew requesting Paramedic Specialist or ACP resource.
    5. Any event requiring clinical support, leadership, or advanced care.

    iv. Any request by CliniCall to have a Paramedic Specialist/ Tango unit attached to a call must not be reasonably denied. Collaboration between CEMD and CliniCall should be considered to ensure resources are assigned appropriately.

    v. Paramedic Specialists may be dispatched to provide crew support and education, and to support the strategic goals of BCEHS, including the following situations:

    1. Clinical/Technical debriefings of crews following HALO, HRH and/or CBRNE events.
    2. BCEHS Clinical Trials, projects, and proof of concept to support the development of practice and improving patient care.
    3. Continuing education campaigns – in conjunction with BCEHS Learning and the Paramedic Practice Educators.

    vi. While conducting clinical pre-briefings and/or de-briefings, Paramedic Specialists must ensure the activation of the Critical Incident Stress (CIS) response team where appropriate.

    c. Paramedic Specialist Dynamic Deployment

    i. Paramedic Specialists must be deployed in PRU mode as the standard baseline.

    ii. At the start of each shift and throughout CliniCall, in collaboration with CEMD, must identify any unstaffed advanced care paramedic resources.

    1. Paramedic Specialist resources must be utilized to provide operational coverage in these areas and will be deployed in PRU mode.

    iii. Once operational resource vacancies are filled, and after consulting with CliniCall and CEMD, the Paramedic Specialist must dynamically deploy to enhance ACP resources where required. Priority is given to channels that lack ACP-PRU resources during the shift.

    1. Start locations may be modified by the CEMD in consultation with CliniCall based on operational needs at the time of deployment.

    iv. It is normal practice for Paramedic Specialist resources to move around Metro Vancouver (between channel boundaries) as event volume and locations naturally vary.

    1. When crossing channel boundaries, the Paramedic Specialists must contact the appropriate channel operators.
    2. If crossing Dispatch Operations Centre boundaries, the Paramedic Specialist must coordinate with the appropriate CEMD(s) and consider advising Provincial Operations Manager (POM).

    v. If a large-scale event occurs, either in Metro Vancouver or provincially (ie: Tumbler Ridge, Lapu Lapu), crews can respond from CliniCall to PRU; or PRU to CliniCall to provide support for the team. This must be done in team collaboration to ensure resources are deployed appropriately.

    d. Paramedic Specialist Unit Status

    i. It is an expectation that Paramedic Specialist resources will have logged in and departed Station 287 (Virtual Way) for their dynamic deployment area no later than 45 minutes after the commencement of their shift.

    1. If a Paramedic Specialist is unable to deploy at this time due to equipment check, mentee orientation or as otherwise necessary, they will go Out of Service – Admin.

    ii. Paramedic Specialist response units (callsign “Tango”) can be deployed in Paramedic Response Unit (PRU) or Tango (TNG) status depending on required status and system pressures.

    1. The default unit status for Paramedic Specialist Response units must be PRU mode.
    2. The first car to leave Virtual Way at 0600H and 1400H shift times may request to be placed in TNG mode provided they are a lone responder with no partner or student/mentee, and they have not been assigned ACP regional coverage.
    3. The remaining Paramedic Specialist Response units can be manually changed to TNG mode when system pressures or high-risk events including mass gatherings, protests, holiday celebrations, etc. are occurring, OR
    4. For a dedicated response to specific Paramedic Specialist duties as listed in section 2(b) 1-5, as determined in collaboration with CliniCall.

    TNG Mode

    In TNG mode, the PS resource does not present in Recommend Unit to ensure availability for TNG-specific events. Members must remain extra vigilant in scanning queues and informing dispatch of appropriate responses (e.g., nearby cardiac arrest).

    iii. When appropriate, and in consultation with the PS resource and CliniCall, the Channel Operator must change the Paramedic Specialist deployment status from PRU to TNG.

    iv. When mentoring or paired, the PS must stay in PRU mode unless specifically tasked with a TNG event.

    e. Paramedic Specialist Dispatch & the Clinical Response Model (CRM)

    i. The Clinical Response Model (CRM) determines which MPDS event types are targeted for a Paramedic Specialist response.

    1. A Paramedic Specialist in PRU mode will respond to events as outlined in the dispatch procedures for PRU.

    ii. When the PS resource is in TNG mode, response recommended by the CRM must be dispatched according to the following criteria:

    CRM indicates 'TNG' or 'Consider TANGO':

    1. Paramedic Specialist response is indicated within a 45-minute geographical response window (TNG Mode).
    2. This response may be exceeded by the CEMD or stood down at the discretion of CliniCall.
    3. Any decision not to send a resource must be clearly documented by CliniCall.

    CRM indicates PURPLE (non-TNG):

    1. A Paramedic Specialist in PRU mode must be dispatched to all PURPLE events when they are the closest advanced care resource.
    2. A Paramedic Specialist in TNG mode must be dispatched to CRM PURPLE events when they are the closest advanced care resource.

    CRM indicates RED (non-TNG):

    1. A Paramedic Specialist in PRU mode must be dispatched to all RED calls when they are the closest advanced care resource.
    2. A Paramedic Specialist in TNG mode must be dispatched to CRM RED events in consultation with the PS on-car and CliniCall based on operational need.

    CRM indicates ORANGE (non-TNG):

    1. A Paramedic Specialist in PRU mode must be dispatched as per current PRU dispatching guidelines.

    2. A Paramedic Specialist in TNG mode must not be dispatched on CRM ORANGE events unless there are extenuating circumstances identified in consultation with CliniCall.

    3. iii. Additional calls requiring a Paramedic Specialist response may be identified by CliniCall, Dispatch Supervisor, CEMD, Dispatcher or Paramedic crew irrespective of CRM or MPDS. The unit should be assigned when determined to be appropriate by CliniCall, in discussion with the Charge EMD.

    Lone Responder Safety Protocol

    Aligning with the Lone Responder safety plan, the Paramedic Specialist must be dispatched concurrently with a PCP crew Code 3, or Fire Department if unavailable.

    iv. Where conflict arises with the CRM or when otherwise determined necessary, CliniCall must coordinate with the CEMD to determine Paramedic Specialist deployment on a call-by-call basis.

    1. This includes those events requiring a Paramedic Specialist as an additional layer or resource but not captured by the CRM, as well as ‘Paramedic Specialist Initiated Events.’
    2. CliniCall must ensure they clearly document these instances.
    3. The Charge Dispatcher must coordinate the operational and dispatching implications with the respective channel operator.

    Assignment Request

    A Paramedic Specialist may also request to be assigned to a call through a request to their channel operator if they deem that a call may be appropriate for PS deployment.

    f. Shift Times

    i. It is the expectation that a Tango resource remains in service until the completion of their end of shift time.

    ii. Out of Service-Admin requires a reasonable explanation and must be requested through your channel operator or OCPS depending on the ask.

    iii. Tango members may return to Station 287 (Virtual Way) 30 minutes prior to shift end but must stay available by portable radio until completion of shift.

    iv. The expectation of the dynamic deployment model is that meal breaks and restocking shall be completed at the nearest ACP station, unless extenuating circumstances arise (ie: personal items required, soiled uniform).
